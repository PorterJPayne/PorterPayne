Welcome to my Blog!

I have decided that I need a place to document all of my engineering projects.
Although I find notebooks to be incredibly useful for getting my ideas out of my head,
they lack organization (at least in the way that I use them).
I find things like Obsidian a bit obnoxious to use. I understand that that could be an upsetting opinion.
I really do wish I could get it to work for me, but it just doesn't fit my brains own filing system, I suppose.

And so, the motivation for this site, and the projects that it will contain, is the idea that I know what works for me,
so I should just make it myself.

I am not yet an engineer of any kind. Not electrical, or software, or manufacturing, or mechanical.
But, I am going to try to be all of those things. I am going to try to learn as much about all of those things as I can.
I believe that we are capable of a lot more than we give ourselves credit for and that we live in an age where we can learn to become anything.

I hope this goes somewhere great and I'm so happy you're here.
